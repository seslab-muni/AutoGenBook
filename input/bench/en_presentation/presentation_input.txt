Title: From Calculating Machines to Stored Programs
Summary: A short talk for first-year students on how programmable machines became general-purpose computers: Babbage's engines, punched-card programs, and the stored-program idea of the EDVAC report and the Manchester Baby.
Audience: first-year computer science students
Duration: 10 minutes
Slides: 6
Style: clear, visual, few words per slide
Must include: the difference between a programmable calculator and a stored-program computer
